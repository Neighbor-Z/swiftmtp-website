How can we help?

Find answers to frequently asked questions and troubleshooting guides for SwiftMTP.

Why is my device not being recognized?

Ensure that your Android device is unlocked and set to "File Transfer" or "MTP" mode in its USB settings after plugging it into your Mac. Some devices default to "Charging Only" when connected.

How does the AI Natural Language Search work?

The Natural Language Search uses AI to interpret your everyday search queries and map them to file paths or metadata on your device. Ensure you have enabled and configured the AI Mode in the Settings panel for this feature to work.

Is SwiftMTP compatible with Apple Silicon?

Yes, SwiftMTP is distributed as a Universal Binary, which means it runs natively at full speed on both Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3) and Intel-based Macs.

Where can I report a bug or request a feature?

We welcome your feedback! Please visit our GitHub Issues page to report any bugs you encounter or suggest new features, or email directly.
